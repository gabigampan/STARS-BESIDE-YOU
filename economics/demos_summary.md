# Technology demonstrations on the Moon and Mars vs. tests on Earth: summary

Files: `data/space_demos.csv` (22 rows) and `data/space_earth_pairs.csv` (8 pairs). All figures are in nominal dollars of the year indicated, not adjusted for inflation. The CSV flags which rows were only seen in a search excerpt (`verified = no`).

## Main idea
Demonstrating an artifact on the Moon or Mars costs **between 3 and 40 times more** than building and testing it on Earth, according to the pairs that can be documented. Just transporting 1 kg to the lunar surface (1.2–1.5 M USD/kg) costs the same as **20 to 70 weeks** in a thermal vacuum chamber.

## Key costs of demonstrations in space
| Artifact | Cost | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Sojourner (Pathfinder rover) | 25 M USD (cap) | success |
| Pathfinder lander and airbags | 150 M USD (development cap) | success |
| Ingenuity | 80 M construction + 5 M operation | success (72 flights) |
| MOXIE | ~50 M USD (principal investigator's figure) | success |
| Deep Space 2 | 29.2 M USD | failure |
| MarCO (2 CubeSats) | 18.5 M USD | success |
| CAPSTONE | 23.65 M USD (13.7 + 9.95 for launch) | success |
| PRIME-1 (CLPS delivery) | 50.0 M USD (press: 62.5 M) | demonstration not completed |
| Nokia lunar 4G / Grace hopper | 14.1 M / 41.6 M USD (Tipping Point) | partial / not completed |
| Iris (CMU) | ~0.8 M USD | did not arrive |

**Per-kg rates on the lunar surface:** Astrobotic 1.2 M USD/kg (lunar orbit 0.3 M; aboard a rover 4.5 M). ispace 1.5 M USD/kg. CLPS IM-3 implied ≈0.85 M USD/kg (own calculation: 77.5 M / 91.6 kg).

## Earth tests with a known cost
- Manufacturing and testing of the TRIDENT drill: 7.61 M USD.
- VSAT prototypes with environmental testing: 19.4 M USD for 3 prototypes. Additional VSAT/LunaRay test: 1.1 M USD.
- JSC 20-foot vacuum chamber (agreement with Sandia): 1,189,812.61 USD for up to 5 one-week tests/year, about 58,000 USD per test (estimate).
- Liquid nitrogen for JSC Chamber A: about 10,560 USD/day (2012).
- Pathfinder airbag system (ILC Dover): 4 M USD.

## Pairs (ratio = cost in space / cost on Earth)
TRIDENT 6.6× (direct pair) · Pathfinder airbags 37.5× (proxy) · Ingenuity 3.7× (weak proxy) · MOXIE 12.6× (proxy) · VSAT 18.6× (space side hypothetical) · Iris 3× (estimate) · 1 kg on the Moon vs. 1 week in a NASA chamber 20.5×, or vs. a university chamber 68.6×.

## Gaps
- Not published: the cost of CADRE, of Lunar Flashlight, of the Rashid rover, and of Pragyan separately.
- Nor the stand-alone cost of the Earth campaigns for Ingenuity (JPL 25-foot simulator), the airbags at Plum Brook, CADRE, Glenn's SLOPE laboratory, or the ExoMars tests in Tabernas or the Atacama.
- JPL's MESA rates page, which would give the daily cost of its chambers, was not accessible.
- There is no cost for surface fission power studies or for the purchase of Lunar Outpost's MAPP.
- Most pairs are proxies or estimates. Only TRIDENT is a direct pair of the same artifact.
